## 1 · "Done," in the owner's words
> I open the app and my pieces have their own place. I hit ✎ write and get a real writing page: title, subtitle, my words. I can set a word target, a due date, who it's for — and sort my pieces by date. I can lock a piece so reading can't smudge it. Everything saves as I type, tells me so, and never loses a word. **Nothing about my boards or bits feels any different.**
