## 2. Findings — ranked, each awaiting the owner's verdict

### F1 ❌ The philosophy still promises links, backlinks, and a graph-of-links — v1 deleted them
`philosophy.md` §"How thoughts connect" reads: *"Two mechanisms: Tags… **Links — one thing pointing at another.** From these come **backlinks** and a **graph**."* The links ruling (agreements §6) deleted links from v1, knowingly and with a re-entry gate — but the philosophy wasn't rewritten (only the privacy line was). **Consequence:** the seed-of-why and the seed-of-what now disagree on a core mechanism. **Fix:** the owner rewrites that section in their own voice (e.g., *"thoughts connect through shared words and shared places; a direct thread may come later if I ever miss it"*) — or rejects the finding and reopens links.

### F2 ❌ The philosophy's "Everything is a canvas — there aren't different kinds of thing" contradicts the ruled two-kind model
§"Everything is a canvas" declares one primitive; the ruled model has two (bit + board) and has since D-018. This was superseded ages ago but the prose was never touched. **Fix:** owner rewrites (the section's *spirit* — a canvas can be a caught line or a composed page — survives fine as "a board can be nearly empty or full"), or blesses it as poetry with a note.

### F3 ⚠ "Grow my thinking over time" got thinner when links died — is what remains enough?
The philosophy's core verb is *grow*: an idea "planted, returned to, connected to others, slowly develops." The old design's growth mechanic (the lineage/backlinks panel) died with links. What v1 actually offers for watching a thought develop: its **travel across boards** (derived from placement timestamps), its **accumulating pulls**, its **edit-in-place** (with no history, by ruling), and `stage` (unruled, board round). **Question for the owner:** is travel + accumulation + revision genuinely "growth" *for you* — or does this deserve a designed answer in the board round (stage is the natural place)? Not a gap in coverage; a gap in *confidence*.

### F4 🔶 The morning-pages writing experience is deferred with document mode — v1 serves it only via canvas
The owner's founding frame ("info begins as tidbits… my morning page… I draw a cartoon and later want it in an archive") describes *writing downward with inline drawing* — which is document mode, explored and deferred (§6b). In v1, morning pages happen on a canvas board (usable — the prototype proves it — but it's arranging, not writing). **For re-blessing:** accept canvas-morning-pages for v1, or re-weigh document mode's priority against its known costs (split/merge semantics etc.).

### F5 ❌ Export is promised everywhere and scheduled nowhere
"I own all of it, always" is a philosophy promise, agreements principle 10, and outcomes row 8 — and appears in **no phase of ROADMAP** (only the *backup* is scheduled, which is disaster-recovery, not owner-facing export). This exact flag was raised in the retired vetting doc and survives its file. **Fix:** give `/export` a phase (recommendation: it ships with the port or immediately after — it's small, and it's the trust floor under "real notes live here now").

### F6 ❌ ROADMAP and SPEC are now stale against the agreements — known, but list it formally
ROADMAP Phase 2 still says "backlinks · forward-links"; SPEC still carries `kind`, link plans, start-private RLS. Both are queued for the translation step — this finding just makes the sync list explicit so nothing silently survives: **ROADMAP** (phase 2 rewrite, export scheduling, connector batch), **SPEC** (rebuilt from agreements), **migration** (derived fresh, one clean file, empty-DB replace).

### F7 ⚠ Small ambiguity: is the abridged (small) rendering available on the v1 canvas?
The display-size value ("a placement rendered small — first words, tap to jump") was ruled in the document-mode discussion; document mode deferred. Does the v1 *canvas* offer the small card? It's cheap and useful (a compact pointer-card on a busy board) — but currently ambiguous. **One-line ruling needed** (recommendation: yes, it's just a display value; costs nothing).

### F8 🔶 The PWA ("a web app I can install, so it feels like my app") is a philosophy promise currently living in the treats list
Conscious deferral (it matters most with phone capture, Phase 5) — but it *is* a "How I reach it" promise, so it should be deferred by the owner's eyes, not by inertia. Re-bless or re-slot.

### F9 ✅⚠ Known-open cells, formally listed so the audit is complete
`stage`'s shape · the **home surface** (what opens when you open the app — unruled anywhere, and it's the first thing you'll ever see) · the subtype vocabulary's home · the pull's name. All queued for the board round — the home surface is the one most easily forgotten and most felt.
